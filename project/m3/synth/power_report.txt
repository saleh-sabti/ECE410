## Notes
Clock dominates at 57.3% (12.1 mW). This is expected: the 20 ns clock drives
4,139 flip-flops (2×128×16 shift register + control registers). The MAC tree is
purely combinational and contributes only 3.5% of total power.

Power at other corners is not available because routing did not complete;
multi-corner power analysis requires a routed netlist. At ss corner power would
be lower; at ff corner slightly higher.

For M4: reducing N from 128 to 64 halves the register count and is expected to
reduce sequential and clock power by roughly 50%, bringing total below 12 mW.
